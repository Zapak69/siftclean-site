Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

In short: SiftClean scans and cleans your disk entirely on your computer. Your files, their names and their paths are never sent anywhere. If you allow it, the app shares a few anonymous counts that help us understand how it is used.

Data that stays on your computer

Everything SiftClean needs to show you your disk – folder names, file sizes, modification dates and Smart Scan results – is processed locally and kept only in memory while the app is running. Your settings are stored in a small file in your user profile.

Anonymous statistics

When “Share analytics” is on, SiftClean sends the following information:

DataWhy
A random installation ID created on first launchTo count how many installations use the app, without knowing who you are
The type of event: app opened, scan finished or items deletedTo understand which features are used
The amount of space freed and the number of items deletedTo show how much space SiftClean saves in total
The number of files in a finished scanTo improve scanning speed on large disks
Operating system, app version and app languageTo decide where to focus fixes and translations

We never collect file or folder names, paths, file contents, your name, e-mail address or your account details. We do not store IP addresses.

Your choice

You can switch statistics on or off at any time in Settings → Share analytics, or during the first-run setup. When it is off, the app sends nothing.

Where the data is stored

Statistics are received by a Google Apps Script service and stored in a Google spreadsheet operated by us. Google acts as our hosting provider and processes the data under its own terms. Because the data is anonymous, we can’t link it to you or to a specific device.

This website

The website does not use cookies or third-party trackers. When you click a download button, we count the click together with the chosen platform. Nothing else is recorded.

Retention

Anonymous statistics are kept for as long as they are useful for improving SiftClean and may be deleted at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
